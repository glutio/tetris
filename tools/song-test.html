Format
  • melody: notes separated by spaces. C D E F G A B = octave from C5, lower case = octave up, trailing , = octave down (A,). ^F sharp, _B flat (no key signature). Length in eighths after the note: E=1/8, E2=1/4, E4=1/2, E1.5 dotted, E.5=1/16. z2 = rest.
  • chords: name:eighths. Am = minor, G7 = seventh, others major. Must add up to the melody length; total should be a multiple of 4 eighths.
