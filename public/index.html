How to play

Fit the falling blocks into full rows to clear them. Watch out for the special Thai letter, heart and TAK pieces – they crumble like sand when they land.

  • Touch: tap to rotate, drag to move, drag down to speed up, flick down to drop
  • Keyboard: ← → move, ↑ rotate, ↓ down, Space drop, P pause
